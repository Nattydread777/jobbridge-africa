JOBBRIDGE AFRICA — FUNDER ONE-PAGER
Prepared: 19 November 2026

OVERVIEW
JobBridge Africa is an employment platform focused on connecting talent in Africa with job opportunities, employer application channels, and career resources.

MISSION
The platform supports the aims of United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth. JobBridge Africa is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the United Nations.

PLATFORM MEASUREMENT
Current active job listings, job-seeker profile counts, and profile-country counts are published at https://jobbridgeafrica.com/sdg-impact. Those figures are dated and generated from active job records and job-seeker profiles in the application database. Job placements are not currently recorded and are not represented as a measured outcome.

PARTNERSHIP
The platform references the Royal Merchant Multipurpose Cooperative Society Ltd Ughelli as a cooperative education-support partner. Program eligibility, KYC, course approval, guarantor requirements, fees, and repayment terms are controlled by the cooperative's written agreement. Training or funding does not guarantee employment.
